Membership Terms

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Membership & payment

  • Membership costs £10 per month, arranged directly with your coach after you join (standing order or bank transfer — no payments are taken through the app).
  • There is no contract or minimum term. Cancel any time by telling your coach; access ends at the close of the period you've paid for.
  • We may suspend or remove access if payment stops, with a reminder first.

Who can join

  • You must be 18 or over to join.
  • You must complete the health questionnaire (PAR-Q) honestly when signing up.

Training safely — the important bit

  • The Club provides online programming and guidance only. No in-person assessment, instruction or supervision is carried out.
  • All training is undertaken at your own risk. Programmes are general guidance, not medical advice.
  • If you answered YES to any PAR-Q question, or you are in any doubt about your health, you must obtain your doctor's advice before training, and only start once cleared.
  • Stop exercising immediately and seek medical advice if you feel pain, chest discomfort, dizziness or anything unusual. In an emergency call 999.
  • You are responsible for training within your limits, using safe equipment and a safe training space.

Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Club accepts no liability for injury, loss or damage arising from your training, except where caused by our proven negligence. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

Your account & the community

  • Keep your password private; your account is for you alone.
  • Club features (leaderboard, feed) are opt-in. Be decent to other members — we may remove content or accounts that aren't.
  • We may update the app and these terms; material changes will be flagged in the app.

Your data

How we handle your information — including your health answers — is set out in the Privacy Policy.
